# Repository layout

- README.md: purpose and runnable entry points.
- AGENTS.md: product and development authority.
- src/: Guide implementation.
- bin/: public operator commands.
- corpus/: reviewed public source manifest and provenance.
- eval/: separate evaluator and public development cases.
- tests/: isolated synthetic contract tests.
- docs/: API/CLI contracts and operational explanations.
- plans/: dated future work, including deferred Harness² scope.
- proof/: compact sanitized text evidence, exact source and baseline results.
- runs/: ignored generated runs and reports.
- .cache/: ignored downloaded public snapshots.

Do not commit downloaded dependency trees, raw model logs, credentials, private
archives, secrets, or large generated corpora. Public source remains at its
owner; record immutable references and verify cache contents. Do not copy
unlicensed datasets. No cleanup of another task's files or processes.

Selected future screenshot/video proof belongs in the DinkusKit PR-media shelf;
keep authoritative text in this repo. This initial CLI task needs no media.
